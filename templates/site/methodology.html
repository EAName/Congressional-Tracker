Data sources

House roll calls
U.S. House Clerk EVS XML (clerk.house.gov), member-level Aye/No positions resolved to Bioguide IDs.
Senate roll calls
Senate LIS XML (senate.gov). LIS member IDs map to Bioguide via a national congress-legislators crosswalk.
Delegation roster
unitedstates/congress-legislators YAML. Virginia seats are SCD2 on (bioguide_id, term_start).
District geography
config/districts.yaml stores map_version 2021 and 2026. These pages are keyed to {{ map_version }}.
Impact tags
Deterministic rules in config/impact_rules.yaml (classified_by = RULE). Human promotions win. Naked LLM tags never appear here.

Publication gates

District vote cards require a human plain_language_summary under 25 words. We never fall back to vote_question.

Suppressed on district pages (audit sheet only): PROCEDURAL, CLOTURE, NOMINATION, SUSPENSION, MOTION_TO_RECOMMIT.

How to read the heatmap

Each cell is Yea count / Nay count for that member on votes carrying the column’s impact tag. Green means mostly Yea; red mostly Nay; amber split; em dash means no tagged votes yet. Counts are recomputed from the warehouse at render time.
