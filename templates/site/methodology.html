Tags

Seven non-exclusive impact tags come from a versioned rulebook in config/impact_rules.yaml (confidence 1.0, classified_by = RULE). Optional LLM assist never ships to this site until a human promotes the row.

Excluded from district pages

PROCEDURAL, CLOTURE, NOMINATION, SUSPENSION, and MOTION_TO_RECOMMIT stay on the Sheets audit tab. They are real votes and rhetorically indefensible as policy evidence, which is the worst combination to hand a volunteer.

Plain language

Every vote on a district page has a human-written plain_language_summary under 25 words. We never publish the raw parliamentary vote_question; if a summary is missing, the build refuses that row.

Sources

House Clerk EVS and Senate LIS. Each claim carries an inline source link. Last refresh is stamped in the footer as generated_at_utc against a live corpus count of {{ corpus_vote_count }}.

Map version

These pages are keyed to map_version {{ map_version }}. Mixing 2021 voting geography with 2026 targeting attributes a vote to the wrong electorate.
